Frequently Asked Questions (Jeep Wrangler 4xe Plug-In Hybrid)

How long does it take to charge a Jeep Wrangler 4xe Plug-In Hybrid at home on Level 2?

With a 17.3 kWh usable battery and 7.2 kW (32A) onboard AC charger, a daily 20% to 80% top-up (10.4 kWh) takes approximately 1.6 hours, while a 0% to 100% full charge takes about 2.6 hours.

What size wire and breaker do I need for a Jeep Wrangler 4xe Plug-In Hybrid home charger?

To achieve the maximum 32A (7.2 kW) charging speed, install a hardwired EVSE on a 40A 2-pole 240V breaker using 8 AWG Copper THHN / NM-B Wire. If using a NEMA 14-50 plug on a 50A breaker, set the charger to 40A continuous (9.6 kW).

How much does it cost to charge a Jeep Wrangler 4xe Plug-In Hybrid at home?

At the US national average residential electricity rate of $0.16 per kWh, charging the Jeep Wrangler 4xe Plug-In Hybrid from 20% to 80% (10.4 kWh plus ~8% charging loss) costs approximately $1.81, or $3.01 for a complete 0%–100% charge.
